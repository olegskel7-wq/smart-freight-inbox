How rates reach the AI draft

When an enquiry (including parsed attachments) mentions both the origin and the destination of a stored, non-expired rate, the sell price is injected into the drafting prompt — the model quotes a real price instead of a placeholder. The margin itself is never shown to the model or the customer. Expired rates (red date) are kept for reference but never quoted.
