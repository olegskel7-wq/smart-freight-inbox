How these numbers are counted

Ticket cards count emails received in the selected period; reply cards (including average response time) count send events recorded in the period (a reply sent today on an older ticket counts as today's work). Response time runs from email receipt to the first reply on the ticket. SLA breaches are permanent — a late reply stays a breach in the history.
