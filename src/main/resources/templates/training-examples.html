How the feedback loop works

Every time you edit an AI draft before sending, the change is captured as a training example — the customer's enquiry, the AI's original draft, and the reply you actually sent. The system finds the most similar past corrections and shows them to the AI when drafting new replies, so it gradually learns your style. Delete any example that should not be learned from. Trivial edits (a typo fix) are ignored.
